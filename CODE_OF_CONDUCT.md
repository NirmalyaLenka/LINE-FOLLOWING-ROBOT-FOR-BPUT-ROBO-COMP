# Code of Conduct

## Expectations

- Be respectful and constructive. Critique code, documentation and ideas, not people.
- Assume good faith. Beginners and experienced builders share this repository.
- Keep discussion on topic: the robot, its software, its documentation.
- No harassment, discrimination, personal attacks or deliberate disruption.
- Do not publish other people's private information.

## Safety

This project involves batteries and moving machinery. Do not encourage unsafe practices in issues,
pull requests or discussions.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide or remove comments, issues and contributions that break these
expectations, and may block repeat offenders. Report a problem by contacting a maintainer
